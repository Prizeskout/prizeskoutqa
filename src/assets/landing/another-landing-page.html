QUICK ANSWERS

Built to fit your stack without replacing it.

Is PrizeSkout another restaurant dashboard?

No. The dashboard is the control surface. The core product is a profit intelligence layer that reconciles commerce data and exposes it through APIs, embedded workflows and the PrizeSkout Control Room.

Do we need an API integration to start?

No. A pilot can begin with CSV or exported order, payout and cost data. Once value is proven, the workflow can be automated through supported APIs and connectors.

Will PrizeSkout replace our POS or ERP?

No. PrizeSkout is designed to sit between systems such as POS, ERP, delivery platforms and payout records so finance and operations can see one economic truth.

Does PrizeSkout change prices automatically?

Not unless your team enables that behavior. Recommendations can always require approval, and every action stays within floors, permissions and thresholds defined by the merchant.

What can we measure?

True contribution profit, effective commission, promotion cost, settlement variance, margin leakage, channel economics and recommended corrective actions.
