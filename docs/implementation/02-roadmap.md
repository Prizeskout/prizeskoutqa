# Outcome Roadmap

## P0 — Production evidence collection

**Outcome:** A pilot merchant can provide order, agreement, and settlement evidence automatically and PrizeSkout can show source health honestly.

- P0-001 Productionize forwarding-email intake.
- P0-002 Activate and verify evidence processing and source-pull scheduling.
- P0-003 Complete the first production-grade Foodics read-only adapter.
- P0-004 Build the operational-readiness and Connection Center experience.
- P0-005 Add restricted Gmail and Microsoft mailbox OAuth.
- P0-006 Add Marn, Sapaad, Deliverect, and Grubtech adapters based on merchant demand and permitted access.
- P0-007 Add watched-folder / permitted local connector.

Exit criteria: one approved pilot tenant completes a retained, deduplicated, reviewed, source-attributed reconciliation flow without fabricated coverage.

## P1 — Trusted reconciliation workspace

**Outcome:** A finance user can understand and verify every number.

- Guided onboarding and first-reconciliation checklist.
- Governed metric catalog and source precedence.
- Reconciliation workbench with period, platform, branch, and evidence filters.
- Evidence cards, calculation lineage, mappings, and missing-evidence actions.
- Clear distinction among ingestion failure, mapping gap, insufficient evidence, and financial discrepancy.

Exit criteria: a finance reviewer can reproduce a finding from retained evidence and approved terms.

## P2 — Recovery workflow

**Outcome:** A supported discrepancy becomes a controlled, measurable recovery case.

- Case preparation from findings.
- Deadline, assignment, status, and communication tracking.
- Immutable evidence-pack preparation and approval.
- Merchant-controlled submission recording.
- Recovery amount, outcome, duration, and ROI reporting.

Exit criteria: a pilot case moves from finding to closed outcome with a complete audit trail.

## P3 — Accounting outputs

**Outcome:** Reconciled results can enter finance workflows safely.

- Journal-entry preview.
- Chart-of-accounts and entity/location mapping.
- CSV/XLSX exports first; OAuth/API posting later.
- Validation and merchant approval before posting.

## P4 — Promotion and margin intelligence

**Outcome:** Merchants can prevent unprofitable promotions and explain realized performance.

- Scenario simulator, margin floor, funding split, break-even volume.
- Forecast-versus-actual analysis.
- Contract-rate and fee-drift alerts.

## P5 — Broader intelligence

Only after P0-P4 are reliable: broader Copilot questions, scheduled finance artifacts, benchmarks, and tightly controlled action automation.
